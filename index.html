The Editor Behind The Cut

I'm Ehab, a freelance video editor based in Egypt and working with clients across the UAE and wider Gulf region. My work spans luxury real estate promos, healthcare brand content, and AI-powered video — built entirely to hold attention and convert.

Most of my recent work is faceless brand content: real estate reels built from stock footage, market data, and motion graphics rather than original filming — meaning every result comes down to pacing, sound design, and visual storytelling rather than raw footage quality.
